Quick Gesture Cheat Sheet

🖱️ Virtual Mouse

• Move Cursor: Index Finger UP
• Left Click: Index + Thumb Pinch
• Right Click: Middle + Thumb Pinch
• Scroll: Index + Middle Fingers UP

📊 Presentation Controller

• Next Slide: Swipe Hand Right
• Prev Slide: Swipe Hand Left
• Laser Dot: Index Finger UP
• Air Pen: Index + Middle Fingers UP
• Clear Air Canvas: Open Palm (5 fingers)
• Black Screen: Fist (0 fingers)

🔊 Volume & Brightness

• Right Hand Pinch: Master Volume (0-100%)
• Left Hand Pinch: Display Brightness (0-100%)

🔍 Gesture Zoom

• Spread 2 Hands Apart: Zoom In (+)
• Bring 2 Hands Together: Zoom Out (-)
• Press 'R': Reset Zoom
